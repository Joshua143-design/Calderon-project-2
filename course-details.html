College of Management, Accountancy, and Technology (CMAT)

The College of Management, Accountancy, and Technology (CMAT) bridges the gap between modern business strategy, financial stewardship, and emerging digital technologies. Our programs are designed to prepare students for licensure examinations, corporate leadership, and tech-driven entrepreneurship.

Through hands-on laboratory training, industry internships, and real-world case studies, CMAT students graduate ready to lead organizations, manage financial systems, and build innovative software solutions.

Core Competencies & Highlights

  • Software development, networking, and database systems
  • Financial accounting, auditing, and taxation standards
  • Organizational leadership and strategic operations
  • Business analytics and enterprise resource planning
  • CPA Board Exam review and financial compliance
  • Cybersecurity, cloud computing, and web technologies
  • Entrepreneurship, marketing, and human resource management
  • Industry internships and capstone research projects
