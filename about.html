Shaping Future Leaders in Business, Finance, and Technology

At Kabankalan Catholic College, the College of Management, Accountancy, and Technology (CMAT) is dedicated to providing quality Catholic education that blends academic excellence with real-world industry readiness.

  • Accredited degree programs in Information Technology, Accountancy, and Business Administration.
  • Hands-on training through modern computer laboratories, CPA board review support, and industry internships.
  • A Christ-centered learning environment guided by experienced educators who nurture ethical values, innovation, and community service.
